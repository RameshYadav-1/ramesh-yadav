About Me

Ramesh Yadav is a Senior Technical Superintendent in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad. He is involved in the technical and operational management of the department's teaching laboratories, scientific equipment and laboratory infrastructure.

His responsibilities span laboratory operations, scientific equipment management, procurement and installation, asset management, maintenance coordination, infrastructure development and technical support for teaching and research activities.

He works closely with faculty members, students, technical staff and central facilities to support the effective functioning and continual development of departmental laboratories.

In addition to his technical responsibilities, he contributes to departmental technical systems and digital resources, including the development and ongoing improvement of the Department of Physics website.

Academic Work

Research Interests

Current academic interests include computational and molecular simulation approaches to nanoscale and biomolecular systems, with particular interest in molecular dynamics simulations, nanoconfinement, protein–nanostructure interactions and computational methods for understanding complex physical systems.

There is also an interest in the application of machine learning and data-driven approaches to scientific research.
