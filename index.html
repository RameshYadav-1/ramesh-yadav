Profile

About Me

I am a Senior Technical Superintendent in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad, where I work at the intersection of physics education, laboratory operations, scientific instrumentation and research support.

My professional work is centered on building and maintaining the experimental infrastructure that enables effective teaching and research. I coordinate teaching laboratories, scientific equipment, laboratory development and technical resources, while working closely with faculty members, students and technical teams to translate academic requirements into reliable laboratory solutions.

Alongside my technical responsibilities, I am pursuing doctoral research with an increasing focus on molecular dynamics, computational physics and scientific computing. My research interests include molecular simulation of biomolecular and nanoscale systems, nanoconfinement, protein–nanostructure interactions and the use of computational methods to understand complex physical phenomena.

I am particularly interested in combining molecular simulation, high-performance scientific computing and machine-learning approaches to develop computational perspectives on problems in physics and biophysical systems. This academic work complements my hands-on experience with experimental laboratories and scientific instrumentation, giving me a perspective that connects physical experimentation with computational investigation.
